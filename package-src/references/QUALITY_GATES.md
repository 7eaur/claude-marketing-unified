# Quality Gates

Run the gates appropriate to the deliverable before final output.

## 1. Truth and evidence

- No invented proof, testimonials, numbers, awards, case-study results, scarcity, guarantees, or capabilities.
- Separate verified facts from strategic assumptions.
- If a claim needs current evidence, follow `CURRENT_DATA_POLICY.md`.

## 2. Strategic fit

- The deliverable has a clear objective.
- The message matches the audience and funnel/awareness stage.
- The offer or next action is appropriate to that stage.
- The chosen channel and format support the objective.

## 3. Message quality

- One dominant idea is easy to identify.
- The opening earns attention without clickbait that the body cannot pay off.
- Benefits are concrete rather than generic.
- Features are translated into meaning when needed.
- The CTA is specific and proportionate.

## 4. Brand consistency

- Voice and tone match verified brand context.
- The copy does not switch personalities mid-piece.
- Brand-specific prohibited phrases, claims, or formatting are respected.

## 5. Human quality

For public-facing copy, run `modules/custom/anti-ai-quality/SKILL.md`.

Reject output that reads like generic AI filler, a corporate template, or a list of buzzwords without a sharp idea.

## 6. Channel fit

- Social copy is scannable and native to the intended platform.
- Paid ads match the campaign objective and audience awareness.
- Landing/website copy provides enough information for the user's decision stage.
- Creative direction describes a visual idea, not just text pasted onto a poster.

## 7. Redundancy and discipline

- Remove repeated claims that add no new meaning.
- Do not repeat the same hook in headline, support line, caption, and CTA.
- Do not expose internal framework labels unless useful to the user.
- Do not make the final deliverable longer merely because many modules were consulted.

## 8. Final conversion review

For conversion-heavy ads, offers, and sales copy, use `modules/rob/copychief/SKILL.md` before delivery when applicable.

Apply its critique to improve the work, but preserve verified product/brand facts and the user's explicit constraints.
