# Routing Map

Use the smallest specialist set that can complete the task well. Add playbooks only when they contribute a distinct responsibility.

## Foundation playbooks

### Product / positioning context
Read `references/playbooks/corey/product-marketing/PLAYBOOK.md` when the task depends on positioning, audience, differentiation, value proposition, product context, or messaging foundations.

### Customer research
Read `references/playbooks/corey/customer-research/PLAYBOOK.md` when the task requires voice-of-customer, pains, jobs-to-be-done, objections, buying triggers, research synthesis, or audience discovery.

### Marketing planning
Read `references/playbooks/corey/marketing-plan/PLAYBOOK.md` for campaigns, channel plans, objectives, sequencing, priorities, and coordinated marketing programs.

### Marketing ideas
Read `references/playbooks/corey/marketing-ideas/PLAYBOOK.md` when ideation breadth, campaign concepts, growth ideas, or alternate marketing approaches are needed.

## Content and social

### Content strategy
Read `references/playbooks/corey/content-strategy/PLAYBOOK.md` for content pillars, topic prioritization, editorial strategy, audience questions, and deciding what should be published and why.

### Social media
Read `references/playbooks/corey/social/PLAYBOOK.md` for platform-native social content, hooks, post structure, repurposing, calendars, engagement, and social execution.

### Brand voice
Read `references/playbooks/rebecca/brand-voice-guidelines/PLAYBOOK.md` when defining or enforcing voice, tone, personality, messaging consistency, and channel-specific tone.

## Persuasion and copy

### Marketing psychology
Read `references/playbooks/corey/marketing-psychology/PLAYBOOK.md` when choosing persuasive principles, reducing friction, understanding behavior, or evaluating why a message should work.

### Copywriting frameworks
Read `references/playbooks/rebecca/copywriting-frameworks/PLAYBOOK.md` when selecting or applying structured persuasion frameworks such as problem-solution, attention-interest-action, before-after, feature-benefit, or story-based structures.

### General copywriting
Read `references/playbooks/corey/copywriting/PLAYBOOK.md` for website copy, landing copy, value propositions, headlines, calls to action, and benefit-led messaging.

### Copy editing
Read `references/playbooks/corey/copy-editing/PLAYBOOK.md` when improving clarity, flow, concision, consistency, readability, and polish without changing the core strategy.

## Paid acquisition and creative

### Ads strategy
Read `references/playbooks/corey/ads/PLAYBOOK.md` for paid acquisition strategy, audience structure, campaign objectives, measurement, optimization, retargeting, and channel decisions.

### Ad creative
Read `references/playbooks/corey/ad-creative/PLAYBOOK.md` for creative angles, concepts, hooks, static/video creative direction, variants, and creative testing.

### Ad copy
Read `references/playbooks/rob/ad-copy/PLAYBOOK.md` for direct-response paid-social copy, especially Meta-style primary text, headlines, hooks, funnel-awareness matching, and ad variants.

### Copy chief
Read `references/playbooks/rob/copychief/PLAYBOOK.md` as the senior final review for persuasive sales/ad copy when conversion quality matters. It should critique and improve; it should not replace upstream strategy without a reason.

## Offers and launches

### Offers
Read `references/playbooks/corey/offers/PLAYBOOK.md` when packaging, framing, bonuses, guarantees, pricing presentation, offer structure, or perceived value is central.

### Launch
Read `references/playbooks/corey/launch/PLAYBOOK.md` for product/service launches, rollout sequencing, launch campaigns, pre-launch, launch-day, and post-launch planning.

## Mandatory quality playbook

For public-facing persuasive copy, read `references/playbooks/custom/anti-ai-quality/PLAYBOOK.md` after drafting and before final delivery.

## Common routes

### Organic social post
product-marketing (when context is incomplete) → content-strategy (if topic selection is needed) → social → copywriting-frameworks (only if useful) → anti-ai-quality

### Social content calendar
product-marketing → customer-research (when audience evidence is weak) → content-strategy → social → anti-ai-quality

### Paid Meta ad
product-marketing → customer-research or existing audience context → marketing-psychology → ads → ad-creative → ad-copy → copychief → anti-ai-quality

### Marketing campaign
product-marketing → customer-research → marketing-plan → marketing-ideas as needed → content-strategy / ads / social depending on channels → relevant copy playbooks → quality gates

### Landing page
product-marketing → customer-research → marketing-psychology → copywriting-frameworks → copywriting → copychief when strongly conversion-oriented → anti-ai-quality

### Brand voice system
product-marketing → brand-voice-guidelines → anti-ai-quality

### Offer creation
product-marketing → customer-research → marketing-psychology → offers → copywriting → copychief → anti-ai-quality

## Avoid over-routing

Do not load every playbook because a task is 'marketing.' If the user asks only to polish an existing caption, `copy-editing` plus `anti-ai-quality` may be enough. If the user asks only for campaign architecture, do not load detailed ad-copy playbooks until copy is actually required.
