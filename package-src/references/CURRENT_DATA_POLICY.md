# Current Data Policy

Marketing platforms and market conditions change. Treat time-sensitive guidance as data to verify, not permanent doctrine.

## Verify when current data matters

If tools or browsing are available, verify current information before relying on claims about:

- platform algorithms or ranking behavior;
- recommended posting times/frequencies;
- current ad formats and technical specifications;
- platform feature availability;
- current benchmarks, CPM/CPC/CPA/CTR ranges;
- pricing or policy changes;
- current trends, memes, creator formats, or audience behavior;
- laws, regulations, or advertising policies;
- competitor offers or positioning;
- market statistics.

Prefer official platform documentation for specifications and policies, then strong recent evidence for behavior and benchmarks.

## If live verification is unavailable

Do not present a dated module value as a universal current fact. Use it only as a historical heuristic, label material uncertainty, and prefer timeless principles.

## Separate evergreen from volatile guidance

Evergreen examples:

- match message to audience awareness;
- make the first line earn attention;
- connect benefits to concrete customer outcomes;
- avoid unsupported claims;
- test meaningful creative differences;
- measure against the campaign objective.

Volatile examples:

- exact image dimensions;
- 'best' posting hour;
- algorithm-specific hacks;
- average platform costs;
- feature-specific setup steps.

Evergreen rules may remain authoritative. Volatile claims require freshness checks when they affect the decision.
